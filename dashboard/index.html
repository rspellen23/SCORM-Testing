What the generator can build
The draft is segmented into 10-15-min units, each grounded in adult-learning design. The AI chooses from the full block range as the content calls for it, picking each by learning objective:
  • Teach & present — flashcards, accordions, process & timeline, comparison panels, infographics, charts (from real sourced numbers only), images, and callouts, with optional entrance animations.
  • Check understanding — knowledge checks (single- and multi-select), and graded, completion-tracked interactions matched to the objective: sort into groups (categorize), put in order (sequence), matching pairs, fill in the blank, and label a diagram (drag-and-drop). Assessments can draw from a randomized question bank (a fresh subset of items each attempt), and learners can work through a decision scenario — a realistic situation with choices and feedback.
  • Reflect — an open-response reflection prompt the learner answers in their own words, then self-checks against an author-written model answer and rubric (completion-tracked, not scored).
  • Review as a game — word search, crossword, a spin-the-wheel quiz (game show), a Jeopardy-style category board (quiz board), and a fast speed-streak quiz drill.
You review and edit everything via the SME document before building.
